# Contributing to +AI

Thank you for helping make the `+AI` declaration clear, practical, and open.

## Scope

Contributions should advance the project's purpose: communicating material AI
assistance and identifiable human or organisational accountability. The
specification is a draft, so examples, accessibility improvements, ambiguity
reports, and well-reasoned proposals are welcome.

## Before proposing a change

- Read the [specification](plus-ai/SPECIFICATION.md), especially its purpose,
  canonical notation, and open-use requirements.
- Open an issue before making a substantial semantic change so its impact can
  be discussed.
- Keep unrelated changes separate.

## Pull requests

1. Describe the problem and the intended outcome.
2. Identify whether the change is editorial or changes the meaning of the
   declaration.
3. Update every affected canonical explanation or example consistently.
4. Check Markdown links, headings, terminology, and rendering before
   submitting.
5. Confirm that you have the right to contribute the material under the
   repository's [MIT License](LICENSE).

Do not add personal, confidential, or third-party material without permission.
Do not submit secrets, credentials, or undisclosed security vulnerabilities.

## Specification changes

The words **MUST**, **MUST NOT**, **SHOULD**, **SHOULD NOT**, and **MAY** are
normative in the specification. Changes to their use, the canonical `+AI`
notation, or the declaration's meaning require explicit rationale and a
version update. Editorial changes must not silently alter normative meaning.

All participants must follow the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
