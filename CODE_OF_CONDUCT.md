# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community.
Disagreement about the declaration, artificial intelligence, or accountability
is welcome when expressed in good faith and with respect for people.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and professional.
- Discuss ideas, evidence, and project outcomes rather than attacking people.
- Respect differing experiences, identities, and perspectives.
- Protect confidential information and respect intellectual-property rights.
- Accept feedback and help maintain a safe, collaborative space.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, sexual
language or imagery, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publication of
another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and take any
other action they consider appropriate to protect participants and the project.
To report a concern, contact a repository maintainer privately through GitHub.
Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces and when someone represents the
project in public.
