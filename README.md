Artificial intelligence is becoming part of how we think, write, design, analyse, code, create and communicate.

That collaboration should not be hidden.

Nor should responsibility be handed to the machine.

**`+AI` is a simple declaration:**

# **AI helped. I stand behind it.**

When a person or organisation places `+AI` after their name, they acknowledge that artificial intelligence materially assisted the work and that they accept responsibility for the final result.

**Eric Mourant +AI**

The mark does not mean that AI is the author.

It does not mean that every word was generated by AI.

It does not guarantee that the work is perfect, error-free or independently verified.

It means something simpler and more important:

**AI participated.
A human made the decision to use the result.
A human stands behind it.**

`+AI` is not a warning.

It is not an apology.

It is not a certificate.

It is a statement of transparency and accountability.

AI can suggest.
AI can generate.
AI can analyse.
AI can transform.
AI can challenge our thinking.

But responsibility should remain visible.

As AI becomes embedded in everyday work, the distinction between “human-made” and “AI-made” will become increasingly difficult to describe—and increasingly less useful.

The more important question will be:

# **Who stands behind the result?**

`+AI` answers that question.

It provides a simple way to acknowledge meaningful AI assistance without surrendering human authorship, judgement or responsibility.

The notation is intentionally open.

No registration is required.

No permission is required.

No membership is required.

No particular AI system is required.

Anyone may use it.

Place `+AI` after your name when AI materially helped you create something and you are prepared to accept responsibility for what you publish, send, present or release.

Use it in email.

Use it in documents.

Use it in software.

Use it in research.

Use it in images, presentations, reports, designs, articles, messages and digital artifacts.

Use it wherever the relationship between human judgement and artificial intelligence deserves to be visible.

The notation is small because the behaviour needs to be easy.

The principle behind it is larger:

# **Use AI openly.

Keep responsibility human.**

And when AI helps you create something you are willing to stand behind:

# **Add `+AI`.**

## Project status

The `+AI` specification is a public **draft**. It is intended as an open
convention, not a certification, legal opinion, warranty, or substitute for
disclosures required by law, regulation, publishers, employers, or
professional bodies.
