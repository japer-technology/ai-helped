# Governance

## Purpose

This repository maintains the `+AI` declaration and its specification as an
open, practical convention for AI provenance and human accountability.

## Roles and decisions

Repository maintainers steward the project, review contributions, manage
releases, and enforce the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Contributors
may propose issues and pull requests under [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Maintainers should seek rough consensus on substantial changes. If consensus
cannot be reached, maintainers make a documented decision based on the
project's purpose, clarity, interoperability, and open-use principle.

## Specification stewardship

The current specification is a draft. A change that alters the canonical mark,
the meaning of the declaration, or a normative requirement must:

1. be discussed publicly before acceptance where practical;
2. explain its compatibility impact;
3. update the specification version; and
4. preserve clear access to prior published versions.

Editorial corrections that do not change meaning may be accepted without a
version change. The declaration remains intended for unrestricted public use:
it must not require registration, membership, payment, certification, or a
particular AI provider.
