The **+AI Helped logo** represents the relationship between **human intent, artificial intelligence, and human responsibility**.

Its three interlocking forms symbolize collaboration: a human directs, AI assists, and responsibility returns to the human who chooses to use and release the result. The continuous shape reflects an ongoing cycle of creation, judgement, refinement, and accountability rather than a handoff from human to machine.

The unified form expresses the central principle of **+AI**: AI may contribute materially, but it does not replace ownership of the outcome.

The logo therefore represents more than AI assistance. It represents **transparent collaboration with a clear point of responsibility**.

**AI helped. I take responsibility.**
