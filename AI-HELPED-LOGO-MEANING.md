The **logo** represents a new relationship between human intelligence, artificial intelligence, and responsibility.

Its three interlocking forms are deliberately inseparable. They represent **human intent, AI contribution, and human accountability**—three forces that increasingly operate together in modern work. The human sets the objective, context, values, and direction. AI contributes capability: generating, analysing, transforming, challenging, accelerating, and expanding what the human can produce. Responsibility then returns to the human who decides what to accept, what to reject, and ultimately what to release.

The logo is therefore not a symbol of AI acting alone. It represents **augmentation without abdication**.

Its continuous, looping construction is important. Human–AI collaboration is rarely a simple one-way process where a person asks a question and receives a finished answer. In practice, ideas pass repeatedly between human and machine. A human prompts, AI responds, the human evaluates, AI refines, the human redirects, and the process continues until an outcome is reached. The interconnected geometry expresses that iterative cycle.

Yet the three elements resolve into **one unified shape**. That represents the final artifact: regardless of how many interactions occurred during its creation, someone ultimately chooses to put their name behind the result.

The triangular overall structure gives the mark a sense of stability and balance. Each part supports the others. Remove one element and the meaning changes. Human intent without AI is simply human creation. AI without human accountability is machine output. Responsibility without acknowledging material AI assistance hides part of the provenance. **+AI brings all three together.**

The negative space at the centre is equally significant. It suggests convergence—a point where multiple contributions become one outcome. It can also be read as movement or direction, reinforcing the idea that AI is not the destination; it is part of the process leading toward a human-adopted result.

The colour **#AC43D9** gives the mark a distinctive identity separate from the conventional blues and greens frequently associated with technology. It is energetic, contemporary and intentionally noticeable, allowing the symbol to function both as a standalone identity and as a recognizable accountability mark attached to documents, software, communications, research, images, and other artifacts.

At its deepest level, the logo represents a simple principle:

**Human intent begins the process.
AI expands what is possible.
Human responsibility closes the loop.**

That is the meaning of **+AI**:

**AI helped. I take responsibility.**
