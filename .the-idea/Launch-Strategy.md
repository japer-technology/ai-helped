# Launch Strategy

Launch it simultaneously as a symbol, a hashtag, and an open standard. The foundational rule is: **Meme on top, protocol underneath**.

Here is the exact playbook your documents propose for launching `+AI` effectively:

### 1. Lead with the Meme, Not the Standard

The most important initial decision is to launch *only* `+AI`. Do not explain the deeper verification levels, model names, or provenance grammar at first, as those weaken the meme. Do not publicly call it a "standard" or use dense regulatory language like "Disclose your use of AI". The entire public proposition should simply be the viral sentence: AI helped. I stand behind it..

### 2. Use Email as Your "Trojan Horse"

Don't ask people to support a standard; ask them to perform a visible behavior. The strongest initial adoption mechanism is the email signature. By simply signing off with `Eric Mourant +AI` and hyperlinking the mark to its definition, you create a curiosity gap. People will click it without feeling subjected to a lecture, providing the repeated exposure needed for a new notation to become familiar.

### 3. Remove All Friction (Give it Away)

For this to explode, it must belong to everyone. You must conspicuously state that no permission is required. If people have to register an account, pay a fee, or attribute JAPER Technology, it will dramatically increase friction and kill adoption. It must be absurdly easy to copy and paste.

### 4. Build the 7-Piece Launch Kit

To maximize the probability of this taking off, the documents suggest building these seven assets in this exact order:

1. **A brutally simple landing page** (e.g., plus-artificial-intelligence.org) featuring just the mark, the slogan, and a simple "Copy" button.

2. **Social cards** (`.svg` and `.png`) with the text: AI helped. I stand behind it..

3. **The +AI Declaration:** A simple, one-page PDF.

4. **The Specification:** The rigorous technical foundation (+AI Specification v0.1) kept quietly behind the scenes for those who ask exactly how it works.

5. **A GitHub Repository:** For the specification, assets, examples, and discussions.

6. **An Email/Signature Kit:** Instructions for Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, etc..

7. **A Share Kit:** Social media posts, profile marks, and a secondary GitHub README badge.

### 5. Position JAPER Quietly

Your first public post should not read like a product announcement, but rather a proposal for a social convention. JAPER Technology should sit "very quietly underneath it". It should be positioned as an open initiative originated by Eric Mourant and JAPER, rather than a JAPER product.

By keeping the public face simple, frictionless, and centered entirely on personal accountability, you allow actual adopters to place the mark themselves, building real social proof organically.
